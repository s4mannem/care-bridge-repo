Answering the 4 Judge Questions & 3 Pointers

Why this problem forces a multi-agent architecture

  • Asymmetric Data Boundary (Track 1 vs Track 2): You are forbidden to rank on race and required to audit against it—forcing an Auditor agent with data permissions and context isolated from the Planner.
  • Contradictory System Prompts (Track 1/2 vs Track 3): Analytical agents must hedge, cite scales, and qualify CIs; the Communicator must write warm, 6th-grade bilingual bus-stop flyers. One agent holding both produces hedged flyers and chatty clinical notes.
  • Visible Disagreement & Self-Correction: The Auditor rejects Draft 0 when catchments overlap or grain rules fail, triggering a bounded revision via session.state["non_demographic_feedback"].
  • Answers "Why Not Picked?": Explicitly explains to runner-up neighborhoods why they missed this month's 4-Saturday cut and which nearby stop serves their catchment.
